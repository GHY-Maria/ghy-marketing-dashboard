The bottom line

What's working

  • Tariff urgency is the engine. The same Section 232 / IEEPA topics lead search traffic, email clicks, and webinar demand simultaneously.
  • Targeted email outperforms 2-to-1. Sends to specific client lists double the open rate of mass sends — refund-themed subject lines click best of all.
  • Canada converts best. Roughly 3× the conversion rate of US visitors — Canada-focused campaigns return the most per effort.
  • Webinars reach real buyers. 245 companies, 83% show rate, and an audience dominated by compliance and supply-chain decision-makers.

What to watch

  • Broken links are leaking traffic. The 404 page got 9,803 views this year — a redirect audit would recover it.
  • Search titles under-deliver. 18M Google impressions convert at under 1%. Refreshing titles on the biggest under-clicked pages could add ~45K clicks/year with no new content.
  • Newsletter signups have normalized (~52/month vs the 150/month spike during 2025's tariff shock). Baseline-normal, but worth a signup push while tariff interest is high.
  • LinkedIn followers growing slower (57/month vs 70 in 2025), even as reach and engagement climb.
