The bottom line

What's working

  • Tariff urgency is the engine. The same Section 232 / IEEPA topics lead search traffic, email clicks, and webinar demand simultaneously.
  • Targeted email outperforms 2-to-1. Sends to specific client lists double the open rate of mass sends — refund-themed subject lines click best of all.
  • Canada converts best. Roughly 3× the conversion rate of US visitors — Canada-focused campaigns return the most per effort.
  • Webinars reach real buyers — repeatedly. Three sessions, 968 registrants from ~240+ companies each, an 83% show rate at the measured session, and an audience dominated by compliance and supply-chain decision-makers.

What to watch

  • Broken links are leaking traffic — and it's growing. The 404 page is up to 11,817 views this year (from 9,803 in April), with a 96% bounce rate. A redirect audit would recover it.
  • Search titles under-deliver. The Section 232 page alone earned 400K Google impressions but converts at 1.3%. Refreshing titles on the biggest under-clicked pages could add tens of thousands of clicks a year with no new content.
  • Newsletter signups holding at baseline (~52/month; May came in at 51). Normal — but worth a signup push while tariff interest is high.
