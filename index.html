The bottom line

What's working

  • Tariff urgency is the engine. The same Section 232 / IEEPA topics lead search traffic, email clicks, and webinar demand at the same time.
  • Targeted email outperforms 2-to-1. Sends to specific client lists double the open rate of mass sends. Do more of these.
  • Canada converts best. Roughly 3× the conversion rate of US visitors — Canada-focused campaigns get the most return per effort.

What to watch

  • Newsletter signups have normalized. ~52/month vs the 150/month spike during 2025's tariff shock. That's a return to baseline, not a decline — but worth a signup push.
  • Broken links are leaking traffic. The 404 error page got 9,803 views this year. An audit and redirects would recover that traffic.
  • LinkedIn followers growing slower (57/month vs 70 in 2025), even as reach and engagement climb.
